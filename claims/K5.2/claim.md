# K5.2 — Symmetry zeros under the point group: no flavour-symmetric Lorentz-scalar mass of the light doublet (Majorana or Dirac) without SSB

**Statement.** With the taste-chiral Majorana mass h C_χ on the heavy doublet R (K5.1), every flavour-symmetric
Lorentz-scalar mass term of the light doublet L — the three anti-self-dual plane masses C_asd(comp) (the Majorana LL
masses, the (3,1) = Δ_L direction of the taste SU(2)_L × SU(2)_R) and the four one-link Dirac masses (LR, the (2,2) =
Higgs direction) — is an exact symmetry zero of the sourced action on every box that carries the full hypercubic point group (L_t = L with the
same boundary condition on all four axes), and hence in the infinite-volume limit: its one-point function vanishes at
every coupling unless a lattice (taste) symmetry breaks spontaneously. The symmetries used are site permutations with a
flavour-blind sign field (χ(x) → s(x) χ(gx), s ∈ {±1}^V), so the ε-vertex (s⁴ = 1), the Yukawa and σ terms (s² = 1 on
every on-site bilinear), the κ term and the flavour-blind source are invariant. The light half can be gapped only
symmetrically or by spontaneous breaking, never by a mass term: the lattice form of "no left-handed mass without a Higgs
condensate".

**Scope.** The group of item 5 is the hypercubic point group (its 32-element stabiliser of C_χ); the terms are the
seven flavour-symmetric light masses (from the 10 strings with an antisymmetric site kernel, the 10 of SU(4) ∋ δ_ab).
The six flavour-sextet strings (symmetric site kernel, Λ²(4) of SO(4)) are not covered here: two of them, the on-site
ε(x)χΣχ and the four-link string, are taste-diagonal light masses that the point-group stabiliser does not remove
(averages 1.000 and 0.354). They vanish under the one-site shifts (the group of item 4) and under the flavour SO(4).
The complete statement — all 16 strings, the full group (lattice stabiliser with shifts, and SO(4)), and the symmetry
that removes each term — is K5.12.

**Domain (box shape).** On L³×L_t boxes, and on boxes whose boundary conditions single out the time axis (pppa), the
box symmetry is the cubic group × time reflections. Every element of the hypercubic stabiliser of C_χ that flips the
(0,3) component C_asd(0,3) — the component in the planes of C_χ itself, whose P_L part P_L C_asd(0,3) P_L is the φ_L
direction of the measurements — exchanges a spatial axis with the time axis, so on such boxes the (0,3) light amplitude
⟨φ_L⟩ is an allowed one-point function: a shape-induced finite-size effect, present in the free theory at O(h) and at
the few-per-cent level of the heavy amplitude, identically zero on the hypercubic boxes, read against its free value and
not a mass term. The other two anti-self-dual components and the four one-link Dirac masses remain zeros on 4³×8. The
theorem's content is an infinite-volume statement.

**Numbers.**

1. Signed-permutation symmetries of K (BFS sign fields), 4⁴: all-antiperiodic and all-periodic boxes have 384
   symmetries, 32 fix C_sd = C_χ, of which 16 flip C_asd (witness (0↔1, 2↔3), also a symmetry on aaaa 6⁴, where it maps C₀
   to neither ±C₀); the production box (antiperiodic time only) has 96, of which 8 fix C_sd and none flips C_asd.
2. Free theory, h = 0.5: ⟨χC_asdχ⟩/V = 0 to 1e-12 × the heavy amplitude on aaaa 4⁴ and 6⁴; with the production bc
   V·⟨χC_asdχ⟩/⟨χC_sdχ⟩ = −45.1 / −51.4 / −46.2 at L = 4 / 6 / 8 (constant within 15 %: a 1/V boundary effect).
3. Interacting theory at O(U²), exact on 4⁴ (connected coefficient 4 Σ_{x<y} G_xy³ (G C_asd G)_xy over Z₂): ≈ 1e-18 on aaaa,
   +5.6e-2 with the production bc.
4. Full group (hypercubic × one-site shifts, BFS signs), stabiliser of C_χ, group-averaged norms ‖Ō‖/‖O‖:
   4⁴ aaaa (|G| = 98 304, stabiliser 8192): C_χ 1, C_L = P_L C_asd P_L 0, C_asd 0, C₀ 0;
   4⁴ pppa (24 576, 2048): C_χ 1, C_L 1, C_asd 1, C₀ 0;
   4³×8 aaaa (49 152, 4096): C_χ 1, C_L 1; the other two asd components, their P_L parts, the four one-link masses and
   their L–R parts 0.
   The composites φ_T_R, φ_T_L (pattern C_χ; a composite ⟨T(x) O T(z)⟩ transforms with the same rule) are allowed.
   The free φ_L (flavour sum) at h = 0.5 vanishes exactly where the tool finds a zero: 4⁴ aaaa ≈ 1e-19; 4⁴ pppa
   −3.9e-3; 4³×8 aaaa +5.0e-4 (the heavy amplitude is 0.017).
5. Point group, 4⁴ aaaa (384 elements, stabiliser of C_χ 32): the group averages of the three C_asd(comp) and of
   P_L C_asd(comp) P_L, of the four one-link masses and of their P_L·P_R + h.c. parts are < 1e-12; C_χ and P_R C_χ P_R
   average to themselves. The one-link masses anticommute with Q (they pair L with R) and are scalar masses: on periodic
   4⁴, K − 0.5 C_onelink lifts all 16 corner modes to the single gap 1.000 (next singular value 2.000).
6. Box shape: on 4⁴ aaaa the stabiliser of C_χ splits 16/16 into elements keeping/flipping C_asd(0,3) (and its P_L
   part); every flipping element moves the time axis. On 4³×8 the box group has 96 elements, the stabiliser of C_χ 8, all
   keeping C_asd(0,3) (mean action +1). Free ⟨φ_L⟩ per flavour at h = 2: 6³×12 −0.00085 (5 % of the heavy amplitude),
   4³×8 +0.00050, 6⁴ pppa +0.00081; 0 on 4⁴, 6⁴, 8⁴ aaaa (< 1e-15). At L_t = 2L, h = 2: 4³×8 +5.0e-4, 6³×12 −8.5e-4,
   8³×16 +4.5e-5 — within the L ≡ 0 mod 4 class the value falls 11× from L = 4 to 8 (L ≡ 2 mod 4 boxes carry zone-boundary
   modes of the taste projector and form a separate series).

**Method.** Symmetries: hypercubic site maps and one-site shifts, with the sign field fixed by K on its links
(`symmetry.sign_field`); the group generated, its stabiliser of C_χ, and group averages of the patterns
(`symmetry.classify`); a one-point function ⟨½χᵀOχ⟩ is forced to vanish iff the average of O vanishes. Free amplitudes
from the dense (K − hC_χ)⁻¹ projected with P_L (`analysis.n1inst.free_phi_L`). O(U²) from the exact expansion of the
four-fermion vertex to second order (the |B| = 2 bag sets) on 4⁴.

**Controls.** C_χ and P_R C_χ P_R are invariant (ratio 1) under the stabiliser. The production box has no C_asd-flipping
element and there the free and O(U²) light amplitudes are non-zero, as are the free amplitudes on 4³×8 and 6⁴ pppa: the
tool finds a zero exactly where the amplitude vanishes.

**Caveats.** Exact symmetry statements on finite boxes; the hypercubic-symmetric boxes are the domain of the zero on a
lattice, the infinite-volume theory its physical content. On L³×L_t and pppa boxes the (0,3) light amplitude is a
measured, free-sized shape effect and enters no claim as a mass (K5.4–K5.8 read V⟨φ_L²⟩, the light gap and ratios to
free).

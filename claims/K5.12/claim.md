# K5.12 — Every light Lorentz-scalar mass string (all 16, flavour-symmetric and SU(4) sextet) vanishes under the full symmetry group of the h C_χ action; the point group alone leaves two sextet strings

**Statement.** With the taste-chiral Majorana mass h C_χ on the heavy doublet, every Lorentz-scalar mass term of the
light doublet averages to zero under the symmetry group of the sourced action: the stabiliser of C_χ in the group
generated by the signed hypercubic maps and the one-site shifts, together with the flavour SO(4) left exact by the
flavour-blind source. The terms are the 16 corner-space strings X_S Z_D that anticommute with every corner Γ_μ: 10 with an
antisymmetric site kernel (a symmetric flavour matrix, the 10 of SU(4) ∋ δ_ab: the three anti-self-dual plane masses,
Majorana LL, and the four one-link masses, Dirac LR, with the self-dual planes in the C_χ direction) and 6 with a
symmetric site kernel (an antisymmetric flavour matrix, the sextet Λ²(4) = (3,1) ⊕ (1,3) of SO(4): the on-site
ε(x)χ^aΣ_abχ^b and the four-link string, taste-diagonal; four three-link strings, LR). The flavour-symmetric terms vanish
under the hypercubic point group alone (K5.2); the sextet terms vanish under the shifts and under SO(4), not under the
point group alone: under the 32-element point-group stabiliser of C_χ the on-site string averages to 1.000 and the
four-link string to 0.354. Each light one-point function vanishes at every coupling unless the corresponding symmetry —
the point group, the one-site shift, or SO(4) — breaks spontaneously. The on-site sextet string with Σ = Γ_a (the Yukawa
triplet) is the fermionic staggered-σ density φ_stag, the bilinear form of the antiferromagnetic order parameter: a
non-zero light sextet mass is AFM order.

**Numbers.** (4⁴ pppp and 4⁴ aaaa, h = 0.5, C_χ = (0,3)+.)

1. 16 strings: 10 antisymmetric, 6 symmetric; all anticommute with K; on the periodic box each lifts the 8 light zero
   modes of K − hC_χ (lowest singular value 0.141–0.300 at m = 0.3); 8 are taste-diagonal (the 6 plane strings, the
   on-site and four-link sextet strings) and 8 LR (4 one-link, 4 three-link).
2. Point group 384, stabiliser of C_χ 32; with one-site shifts 98 304 elements, stabiliser 8192 (4096 with an odd total
   shift), C_χ-flipping elements 8192.
3. Point-group stabiliser: the light parts of all 10 flavour-symmetric strings average to < 1e-16; the on-site string
   averages to 1.000 (light part 1.000), the four-link string to 0.354 (light part 0.354 on aaaa, 1.000 on pppp).
4. Full stabiliser with shifts: the light parts of all 16 strings and the 6 sextet strings as whole operators average
   to < 1e-17; the on-site string is flipped by exactly the 4096 odd-shift elements; every sextet string is flipped by
   as many stabiliser elements as keep it. The ℤ₄ extension of the point group (the C_χ-flipping elements composed with
   χ(x) → i^{ε(x)}χ(x), which multiplies every same-parity bilinear by −1) also kills every light part.
5. Flavour: SO(4) has one invariant in 4 ⊗ 4 (δ_ab, symmetric) and none in Λ²(4); SU(2)_σ alone leaves four, all in
   Λ²(4). The Yukawa triplet Γ_a is antisymmetric and commutes with the hidden triplet: SO(4) = SU(2)_σ × SU(2)_hidden.

**Method.** `masspairing.analysis.mass_strings`: each string as the translation-covariant lattice bilinear with phase
ζ_S(x) and displacement D (boundary signs included); light part P_L O P_L (taste-diagonal) or P_L O P_R + h.c. (LR);
groups from `masspairing.symmetry` (signed site maps found on K, closure, stabiliser of C_χ); ‖Ō‖/‖O‖ of the group
average (0 ⇒ an exact symmetry zero). Flavour invariants: null space of the Lie-algebra action on 4 ⊗ 4.

**Controls.** C_χ averages to 1 and C_asd(0,3) to 0 under the full stabiliser. Sabotage: with the shifts dropped (the
point-group stabiliser) the zero assertion fails on exactly the on-site and four-link sextet strings. SU(2)_σ alone leaves
sextet invariants, so the flavour test detects an allowed sextet term.

**Caveats.** Exact statements on 4⁴ boxes with the full hypercubic group; on L³×L_t and pppa boxes the (0,3)
anti-self-dual light component is a box-shape effect (K5.2), and the sextet strings are killed by the shifts there too
(checked in the research notebook on 4⁴ pppa and 4³×8, not asserted here). The theorem's content is an infinite-volume
statement.

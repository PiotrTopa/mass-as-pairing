# K3.5 — the U(1)_ε-exact family exits through a bond crystal or a gapless fermion, never the (10,3,1) channel

**Statement.** On the sign-free link-field model at κ = −0.01, λ = 1, at the six points (y, g₁₀, g₆) = (0, 0.05, 0),
(0, 0.1, 0), (0, 0.05, −0.05), (0, 0.1, −0.1) — the g₆ = 0 line and the −edge at y = 0, where U(1)_ε × SU(4) × one-site
shift are exact and a trivial symmetric gap is excluded by the Lieb–Schultz–Mattis argument — and the two −edge points
(2.41, 0.05, −0.05), (2.41, 0.1, −0.1), on L = 4, 6, 8:
1. the complete χ₁₀ never grows: (6,8) exponent + 2σ ≤ 0.07 at every point, ξ₁₀/L falls from 6⁴ to 8⁴ by more than 2σ,
   χ₁₀ + 2σ < 0.6 × χ₁₀,free at every point and volume (central ratios 0.12–0.49) and ξ₁₀/L ≤ free + 2σ;
2. at g_j = 2g₁₀ = 0.2 (both −edge points with g₁₀ = 0.1) the exit is the columnar bond crystal: V⟨D_∥²⟩ grows with
   (6,8) exponents 2.33(12) and 2.17(10), the link-field dimer length jumps to ξ/L = 0.35–0.53, D_⊥ is flat, and the
   fermion correlator develops a gap; the (6,8) exponents above 2 are a finite-size crossover into the crystal (the
   6⁴ chains are intermittent), not scaling exponents;
3. at the four points with g_j ≤ 0.1 no measured channel has (6,8) exponent − 2σ ≥ 0.5; at the three y = 0 points
   among them the zero-momentum fermion correlator is flat (ρ = 0.976–0.993, free 1): a free-like gapless fermion with
   an irrelevant four-fermion perturbation. At (2.41, 0.05, −0.05) the crystal and the ε channel both grow
   sub-linearly (0.48(7), 0.56(6)): critical-like, not ordered, at L ≤ 8.

**Method.** 18 chains (L = 4: exact propagators, 400 trajectories after 60; L = 6: 1000 after 100, exact propagators
every 4th; L = 8: 1500 after 100, 8 noise vectors every 2nd), trajectory-length jitter 0.3; y = 0 chains from
hot σ and zero link fields, y = 2.41 chains from the staggered σ start. Analysis `masspairing.analysis.k3.ensemble`;
ρ = mean of G_f over the middle odd time slices / mean over the edge odd slices (t = 1, L − 1), jackknifed
(`rho_flat`, `rho_estimate`); G_f(t) is the zero-spatial-momentum correlator from a point source with the η₄ sign.

**Pre-registration and deviations.** Readout and criteria were fixed in the run order before the data were read
(notebook commit 0855fb1): KEEP ⇔ χ₁₀ exponent − 2σ ≥ 0.5 on (6,8) with ξ₁₀/L rising (by > 2σ) at any point; KILL ⇔ a
crystal or 6-channel grows ∝ V (exponent − 2σ ≥ 1 on (6,8), dimer length rising, D_⊥ flat) while χ₁₀ + 2σ < 0.6 ×
free and ξ₁₀/L falls. Verdict: KILL — the (10,3,1) channel loses at every point.

**Controls.** (1) Equilibration ⟨s_j²⟩/2g_j 1.05–1.18; acceptance 0.93–1.00; ⟨e^{−ΔH}⟩ 0.985–1.002. (2) Injected χ₁₀
growth (× (V₈/V₆)^0.8 and ξ₁₀/L × 1.5 at 8⁴ at (0, 0.05, 0)) flips the verdict to KEEP; replacing the 8⁴ crystal by its
6⁴ value fails the crystal clause at both points. (3) With 100 more trajectories cut from every chain no (6,8) exponent
moves by more than 0.084 (the dimer exponent at (0, 0.1, −0.1)) and no verdict changes.

**Caveats.** The on-site 6 (U(1)_ε-breaking) channel has no readout at y = 0: the on-site bilinear n_a(x) =
¼ tr[Γ_a S(x,x)] vanishes configuration by configuration there (the stored series are exactly 0; a dense 4⁴ test with
arbitrary link fields gives 0 at y = 0 and 0.081 at y = 2.41), and its connected susceptibility was not measured; an
on-site 6 condensate at the y = 0 weak-coupling points is excluded only indirectly, by the flat fermion correlator
(ρ ≥ 0.976 at 8⁴ bounds m ≲ 0.08). SU(4) breaking is not measured either. No hysteresis check at the −edge crystal
points. Two volume pairs; 8⁴ χ₁₀ and χ₆ are stochastic (errors 3–10× the 6⁴ ones). χ₁₀'s (6,8) exponent is slightly
positive at the crystal points and at (2.41, 0.05, −0.05) (+0.03(1), +0.05(1), +0.01(2)): the ratio to free rises with
L but stays ≤ 0.5 by extrapolation to L = 12.

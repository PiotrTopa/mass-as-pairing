# I.1 — the RHMC simulates the measure exactly: free limit, forces, reversibility, δτ², exact-determinant Metropolis, 4⁴ phases

**Statement.** The rational hybrid Monte Carlo of the package (`masspairing.action.Model`, `masspairing.hmc.HMC`:
pseudofermions with the Lanczos action and heat bath, Zolotarev / multishift-CG force, Omelyan integrator, Metropolis
step) simulates the weight det D_c(σ) e^(−S_B) of the ε model exactly. (a) At y = 0 on 4⁴ (antiperiodic time) it
reproduces the free staggered doublet: log det(D†D) equals the twisted-momentum sum, the CG propagator equals the
momentum-space propagator, the on-site block vanishes, and the Lanczos and rational matrix functions agree with dense
linear algebra (also at y = 1). (b) On 4⁴ at (y, κ, λ) = (1.7, −0.01, 1) the forces are the gradients of the exact
action, the integrator is reversible to the solver tolerance, the energy violation scales as δτ², ⟨e^(−ΔH)⟩ = 1.
(c) On 2⁴ (all directions antiperiodic, κ = 0, λ = 1) the RHMC agrees with an independent single-site Metropolis
integration that uses the exact determinant (no pseudofermions, no rational approximation, no molecular dynamics), and at
y = 0 both reproduce the exact single-site integral. (d) On 4⁴ at κ = −0.01 the code separates the symmetric phase
(y = 1.0) from the SMG phase (y = 2.5): O₄ grows by a factor 24.5 and σ² grows.

**Method.** (a) Dense 512 × 512 algebra against the sparse and Krylov code paths (`masspairing.solvers.lanczos`,
`multishift_cg`, `masspairing.rational.invsqrt_partial_fractions`). (b) Finite differences of the exact Lanczos action;
forward–flip–backward integration; 10 trajectories per step count on a thermalised configuration. (c) Single-site
Metropolis on σ (step 0.6, one 2 × 2 diagonal block of the dense 32 × 32 operator changes per proposal; det D = e^{log|det D|}
is real and positive on every configuration), measured every 5th sweep, against 2500 RHMC trajectories (6 Omelyan steps,
τ = 1, 200 thermalisation), exact propagator blocks in both; errors from 20 equal blocks. The y = 0 control integrates
the single-site weight exp(−|σ|²/2 − |σ|⁴/4) of a three-component field. The runs of (c) are generated by
`scripts/derive_k7.py mc` (≈ 5 min for the I.1 runs) and stored in `data/derived/k7/mc/`; the check regenerates a prefix
of each (200 sweeps, 40 trajectories after the thermalisation) and requires bit identity. (d) Two 4⁴ chains (seeds 431,
432; 20 steps for the first 15 trajectories, then 10) with exact propagator blocks; errors from 10 blocks.

**Caveats.** (d) is a qualitative single-volume test: on 4⁴ the staggered bilinear is enhanced at y = 2.5
(V⟨|φ_stag|²⟩ = 1.755 against the uncorrelated-site value O₄ = 0.078), as the divergent staggered susceptibility of the
SMG phase predicts on a finite volume; it is not evidence for or against a condensate. At y = 1.2 the fermion determinant
changes ⟨σ²⟩ only at the per-cent level, so (c) tests the determinant mainly through O₄ and the bilinears; I.2 repeats
the comparison at y = 2.5 with a sabotage control.
